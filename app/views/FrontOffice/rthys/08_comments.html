Registration comments

Your comments must be about what you consider to be the project’s main issues and impacts. You should include as much detail as possible and cover anything that may affect your day-to-day life.

This information will be:

  • seen by the Examining Authority
  • published on our website

Your comments should not:

  • use racist, inflammatory or abusive language
  • link to third party websites (you can include links to GOV.UK or websites for chartered professional institutes)
  • contain information relating to children or crime
  • include personal information about yourself or any details that could identify a third party, especially sensitive information
Examples of sensitive information

Sensitive information means any information relating to an individual's:

  • race
  • ethnic background
  • political opinions
  • religious beliefs
  • trade union membership
  • genetics
  • biometrics (where used for identification)
  • health
  • sex life or orientation

Any comments submitted that do not meet our criteria may be returned to you.
