Comments

Date received: 23 Aug 2026

I am concerned about the proposed solar farm near the village of Gainsthorpe because of the potential impact it could have on the local area and community. While I understand the importance of renewable energy, I believe there are several legitimate concerns that should be carefully considered before any development goes ahead.

One of my main worries is the effect on the rural character of Gainsthorpe. The village is surrounded by attractive countryside, and a large solar farm could significantly alter the landscape and views that residents and visitors currently enjoy. This could change the unique character of the area for many years.

I am also concerned about the loss of agricultural land. Productive farmland is an important local resource, and using large areas for solar panels may reduce the amount of land available for food production.
